90-day border clearance challenge

Making AfCFTA implementation more predictable for women-led small and medium enterprises.

Theme: Trade & Investment Format: Evidence-led discussion Time horizon: 90 days
Main discussion question

If a border agency could test one change within 90 days, what should it change to make cross-border trade faster and more predictable for women-led small businesses?

The practical scenario

A regional border post receives repeated reports of duplicated paperwork, unclear fees, long queues, and traders having to make several visits. The participating institutions have agreed to test one operational improvement, using existing authority and a modest implementation budget.

Your task: recommend a focused pilot that can be launched, measured, and reviewed within 90 days.

Structure your contribution

  1. Place and problem

    Name the country, corridor, or border post and describe the operational bottleneck.

  2. One practical change

    Propose one rule, process, information, or coordination improvement suitable for a 90-day pilot.

  3. Accountable actors

    Identify the lead agency and the public, private, or regional partners needed to deliver it.

  4. Evidence of progress

    Provide a baseline, a target, and one indicator that can be measured before and after the pilot.

  5. Inclusion safeguard

    Explain how small and informal traders will be reached without new digital, language, safety, or accessibility barriers.

  6. Learning decision

    State what result would justify scaling, changing, or stopping the pilot after 90 days.

Before you post

  • Keep the recommendation specific enough to test.
  • Distinguish observed evidence from assumptions.
  • Include the perspective of affected traders.
  • Do not disclose personal or commercially sensitive data.
