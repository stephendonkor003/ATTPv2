ATTP POLICY COMMUNITY
Discussion Participation Guide

Purpose
The ATTP policy community supports respectful, evidence-informed dialogue across
Africa. Use each discussion to share practical experience, relevant evidence,
constructive questions, and policy options.

Before contributing
1. Read the main discussion topic and the supporting resources.
2. Keep your contribution relevant to the question being discussed.
3. Explain the source and context of any data or claim you share.
4. Protect confidential, personal, and commercially sensitive information.

While participating
1. Address ideas rather than attacking people or institutions.
2. Use reactions to acknowledge useful contributions without repeating them.
3. Reply directly when your contribution builds on another participant's point.
4. Share discussion links responsibly and preserve the context of the exchange.

Moderation
Some discussions require review before a contribution appears publicly.
Contributions may be withheld when they are abusive, unsafe, misleading,
unrelated to the topic, or disclose protected information.

Thank you for helping build a useful Pan-African policy conversation.
